Dear Holly Cole,

On September 10th/11th, 2026, we have a *New Moon at 18° Virgo*.

This is one of the most auspicious lunations of this year, and a
much-needed breather after the intense Eclipse season.

If the eclipses shook things up, this New Moon gives us the RESET button.
Life goes back on track – which is a very Virgo thing to say.

But there’s a logic in nature, and a reason for why we need Virgo now. Just
like Leo follows Cancer because too much feeling eventually needs
expression, Virgo follows Leo because all that fire can get exhausting and
needs some grounding.

In the past month, we’ve been running on *high Yang energy* – big events,
big breakthroughs, big decisions, thinking, overthinking, weighing options,
reacting, adjusting, moving.
*New Moon In Virgo – Yin And Earth*

That’s why now we need some Yin energy to ground all this motion and bring
us back to ourselves.

Virgo sometimes gets associated with anxiety and overthinking – probably
because of its Mercury rulership – but essentially, *Virgo is an Earth sign*
.

Earth signs ground us. They bring us back to nature, to the body, to what
is tangible and real. Whenever we move through an Earth season, we tend to
reconnect with ourselves and with the world at a very immediate,
common-sense, *I-can-feel-this-in-my-body* level.

Virgo is the mutable Earth sign, so there’s always that impulse to refine
and improve – but not in some abstract way. Virgo works with what is
actually here.

Virgo is the adjustments that make life work better. You may not realize
how badly you needed to get out of the house, feel the sun on your face,
take a walk, and eat something proper – until Virgo makes it obvious.

But Virgo energy is much more than yoga, rituals, or decluttering your
desk. Virgo intelligence can be applied to pretty much anything – big
decisions, complex problems, the question of what to do next.

Virgo Vs Gemini Vs Leo

Ruled by Mercury, Virgo gives us access to a very different kind of
intelligence than Gemini.

Gemini Mercury is fast, mental, and conceptual. Virgo Mercury is practical,
embodied, reality-based. It doesn’t just ask *“What does this mean?”* – it
asks “What actually works?”

If Gemini Mercury rules the brain, Virgo Mercury rules the gut – our
so-called second brain. And this is where some of our most important
decisions are made, before the mind starts to build a whole story around
them.

And most importantly, with Virgo, we don’t just know something. *We know
what to do about it.*

Because we could look at the exact same problem from a Leo,
existential-crisis perspective: *“Where am I going?” “Is this aligned with
my purpose?” “Am I living my truth or betraying my divine essence?”*

OR we can simply take the next most Virgo, common-sense step and deal with
what’s actually in front of us. BAM – problem solved.
*The Moon In Virgo – The Aspects*

And knowing what to do about it is especially relevant at this New Moon,
because the *New Moon in Virgo is sextile Mars*, the planet of action.

The ruler of this New Moon, *Mercury*, has just switched signs and entered
Libra, and is now part of a very nice *Grand Air Trine* with Uranus in
Gemini and Pluto in Aquarius. This means the New Moon itself plugs into a
much larger story of change and new possibilities.

*Venus* has also just changed signs and is now at 0° Scorpio, so around the
lunation, we have 2 of the most personal planets changing signs. Talk about
New Moon, new beginnings energy.

And the Venus shift carries even more weight – not only because Venus
enters Scorpio, a sign where she will spend approximately 3 months (3 times
more than her usual sojourn) due to her upcoming retrograde…

… but ALSO because the upcoming Sun-Venus conjunction, also called the
Venus Star Point, will happen at the same 0° Scorpio on October 23rd.

So there’s something about this New Moon in Virgo that goes beyond just a
monthly reset – it activates the very essence of a story that will continue
to unfold over the next 18 months.
*The Moon In Virgo – A Swimming Race *

And while all of this larger background astrology is happening, the aspect
the New Moon makes to Mars gives us the practical essence of the lunation.

The New Moon at 18° Virgo applies a tight sextile to Mars at 19° Cancer.
Virgo’s ability to master the material world meets Cancer’s intuitive,
instinctive intelligence.

The Sabian Symbol of the New Moon in Virgo is an almost literal description
of this: *“A Swimming Race.”*

A swimming race demands discipline, but you also have to *read the water,
read the other swimmers, adjust your pace, your breath, your next move – in
real time*. You can’t disappear into your head. The race keeps pulling you
back into the moment.

The race here is not about competition for the sake of winning – but the
kind of *total presence* where you are fully ON – responding to what’s
happening, making constant micro-adjustments, and giving the moment
everything you’ve got.

If you reflect back on moments when you were in a race or a competition,
you may remember how naturally you began to function at a much higher
caliber – not necessarily because you wanted to win, but because the setup
required total focus and presence.

But what would our life be like if we lived in that state of flow – and
applied that presence and mastery to every little thing we do?

If we were intentional *about what we do, fully present, fully engaged,
fully ON*? Chances are, our lives would be much more aligned with our full
potential.

Zooming out again at the astrology of the New Moon – the Grand Air Trine,
Venus at 0° Scorpio activating the upcoming Star Point, and a New Moon in
Virgo sextile Mars:

*This is a time to get serious about what it is that we truly, truly want.*

0° Scorpio is the point zero of desire and truth. These are not the
superficial things in life, not the things we think we want because they
sound good, or because other people want them.

0° Scorpio is not about being popular, or making more money. It’s about
something that touches the very core of our desire – the thing underneath
all the other things we say we want.

And from there, from that seed of desire and truth – what can you
practically do – not affirmations, not wishful thinking, not waiting for
the right circumstances – *but* *practically* – to actually get closer to
what you want?

One real, practical step.

Once the race is on, there’s no turning back. And when *desire meets
presence, commitment, and action, you don’t just want it anymore. You go
get it.*
